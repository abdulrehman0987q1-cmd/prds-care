05 — A little practical care

Let's make the cramps
a little less awful.

Gentle ideas, not promises. Take what suits you and leave what doesn't.

01

Warmth

A warm heating pad or warm water bottle wrapped appropriately may help. Keep the heat comfortable, avoid excessive heat, and protect your skin.

02

Gentle movement

A short walk or gentle stretches may help if they feel comfortable. Rest is also completely okay—listen to your body.

03

Fluids & food

Keep fluids nearby and eat regular meals or snacks that suit your appetite. There's no one perfect thing you have to eat.

04

Rest

Try a comfortable position and a slower pace. It's okay to take a break or ask someone you trust for help.

05

Pain relief

Suitable over-the-counter pain relief depends on age, health conditions, allergies, other medicines, and individual circumstances. Follow package instructions; if unsure, ask a qualified healthcare professional or trusted adult.

06

Be gentle with you

Your body isn't a project to fix. You deserve patience and comfort whether you feel productive today or not.

A gentle safety note

Severe or unusual pain, fainting, fever, very heavy bleeding, or symptoms that significantly interfere with everyday life may need medical assessment. Sudden severe symptoms warrant urgent assessment. This page is for general comfort, not diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.
